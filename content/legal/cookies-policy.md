_Last updated: September 2026_

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains which cookies this site uses and why.

## Strictly necessary

This website works without any cookies. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies unless you allow them.

## Analytics

If analytics is enabled and you consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are most useful and how people found us, including visits referred by AI assistants. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies won't affect how the site works.

## Questions

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, please get in touch through our [contact page](/contact). For more on how we handle personal data, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
