_Last updated: September 2026_

This policy explains how Dooster ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## What we collect

When you use our contact form we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, company, website address and the content of your message.

If you accept analytics cookies, we also collect anonymous information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and how visitors arrived. See our [Cookies Policy](/cookies-policy) for details.

## How we use it

We use the details you send us only to respond to your enquiry and, if we go on to work together, to deliver our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We do not sell your data, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

## How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for as long as needed to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

## Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to operate this website and deliver email, including Amazon Web Services. They process data only on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email us using the address on our [contact page](/contact).

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
